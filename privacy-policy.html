Privacy Policy

ArtEraAI (“ArtEraAI”, “we”, “our”) provides an AI image-generation and editing website at arteraai.vercel.app.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. What information you provide

Depending on how you use the website, you may provide text prompts, generation settings, reference images, and images selected for AI Studio operations.

2. How your information is used

Prompts, settings and selected images may be sent to the ArtEraAI server endpoint so the requested AI generation or image operation can be performed. We do not ask users to provide unnecessary personal information to use the image-generation interface.

3. Browser history

ArtEraAI currently keeps a small “Recent History” of generated image data in your browser’s local storage so you can revisit recent results. This browser-local history can be cleared using the Clear History button on the website or by clearing the site’s browser data.

4. Reference images

Reference images are processed in your browser before being sent with an AI request. Do not upload highly sensitive, confidential, or private images unless you are authorized to do so and understand that the image may be processed by the AI service used for that request.

5. Third-party AI processing

ArtEraAI may use third-party AI infrastructure to process image-generation requests. The privacy, security and retention practices of the relevant provider may apply to data transmitted for processing. Users should review the provider’s current privacy documentation before uploading sensitive material.

6. API credentials

ArtEraAI is designed so that the AI provider credential is kept on the server side rather than being placed in the public browser interface. Never share private API keys or other secrets through prompts or uploads.

7. Data minimization

ArtEraAI is designed to minimize unnecessary storage in the browser. However, server-side logs, hosting records, security logs, or third-party processing may exist according to the policies and configuration of the services used to operate the website.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures for the website and API architecture, including HTTPS through the hosting platform and server-side handling of the AI provider credential. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid uploading passwords, financial information, identity documents, or other highly sensitive material.

9. Children and sensitive information

Do not use ArtEraAI to upload another person’s private or sensitive information without permission. Do not upload identity documents, passwords, payment information, or other confidential records.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, AI providers, or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method made available by the ArtEraAI site owner. This page does not collect a separate contact form.
